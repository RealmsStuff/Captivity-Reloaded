# C4C

Faithful cross-platform rules conversion of the C4C DLL. It includes five hearts, the five-times libido ceiling, the original slower pleasure/faster rape-libido rates, retained positive effects after climax, repeat consumable stock, altered strength loss, exact drug changes, and $100 rewards for infusion and non-parent implantation events.

C4C also embedded a distinct revision of Clothing + Shack Overhaul. This profile enables that revision directly: three Boomers per six-wave tier that charge without exploding, one Tank per tier, and one Pink Hound per tier with C4C's 30-second delay. It does not require the standalone Clothing Overhaul profile.

Select **C4C** as the active rule profile after installing the package.
